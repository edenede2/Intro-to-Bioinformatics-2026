# Fixture provenance

All sequences, backbone coordinates, prediction-like values, and training motifs in
`bioapp/datasets.py` and `bioapp/models.py` are original synthetic teaching fixtures,
released under the repository's MIT license. They are not experimental measurements,
clinical data, or pretrained model outputs. The included PDB represents an idealized
C-alpha helix labeled alanine, not a real protein. Coordinate units are illustrative Å.

BLOSUM62 and PAM250 are supplied by the installed Biopython distribution and are not
copied into this repository. Their original references are shown in the lessons.
`Syllabus.pdf` was supplied by the user and is not relicensed by this project.
